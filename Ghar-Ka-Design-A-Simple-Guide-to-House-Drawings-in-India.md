What is in a House Drawing
A house drawing shows the floor plan and room layout. It marks room sizes, wall positions, and door and window places. It shows where the kitchen and bathrooms will go. For two storey homes it also shows stair position and the layout for upper floors. The drawing can include notes for lighting, plumbing, and small Vastu tips.
How to Choose the Right Design
Start by measuring your plot and noting the shape in feet or meters. Make a list of rooms and any special needs like a store room or balcony. Think about how many people will live in the house now and later. Choose a plan that gives good light and air to main rooms. If Vastu matters to you, mark the prayer room and main door direction.
Benefits of a Clear Plan
A clear Ghar Ka Design saves time and money. It helps the builder estimate materials and labour. It reduces mistakes on site and stops costly changes later. A plan also helps place doors and windows to get good sunlight. With a clear plan you can plan furniture and storage too.
Steps After You Get the Plan
Share the house drawing with your builder and ask for a cost estimate. Use the plan to prepare a work schedule and get local permits. If you need changes, update the drawing before work starts. Always keep a copy of the final plan at the site for the team to follow.
