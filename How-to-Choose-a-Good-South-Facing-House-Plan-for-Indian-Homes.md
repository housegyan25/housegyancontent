What a Good South Facing Plan Shows

A good plan marks the living room, bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms, and shaded outdoor spaces. It shows window positions that let in light but reduce heat. The plan gives room sizes and door points so you can plan furniture and storage. It also helps make a material list for the builder and a schedule for work on site.

Steps to Pick the Right Layout

First, note your plot size and which way it faces. This helps place windows and shaded areas. Second, list family needs like a study corner, guest bed, or store. Third, place the kitchen near plumbing lines to cut cost. Put the living room in a spot that gets light but not too much strong sun at midday. Place bedrooms in quieter corners to give rest. If you want a pooja corner, choose a calm spot away from the kitchen. Small choices now save time and money later.

Design Tips for Comfort

Use covered verandahs or overhangs on the south side to block direct sun. Add windows for cross ventilation to let air move through the home. Plant trees or use a trellis to shade the south wall. Choose light colored paint and flooring to reflect light and keep rooms cooler. Use built in shelves to save floor space and keep rooms tidy. Plan sockets and lights where furniture will be placed.

Materials and Budget Notes

Use the plan to list main materials like bricks, cement, tiles, and paint. Ask your builder for an estimate based on the drawing. Check local prices and keep a small buffer for changes. Choosing standard door and window sizes and common tile sizes can save money. A clear plan reduces waste and helps control budget on site.
