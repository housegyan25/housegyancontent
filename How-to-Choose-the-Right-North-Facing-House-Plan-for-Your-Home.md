What a North Facing Plan Shows

A clear plan marks the living room, bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms, and doors. For a north facing plot, the living room can be placed to use steady daylight. The plan shows window placement to bring in light and support cross ventilation. It gives room sizes so you can plan furniture and storage. The drawing also helps make a material list for the builder.

Steps to Pick the Right Layout

Start with your plot size and note the exact direction it faces. This helps place windows to get the best light. List family needs such as a study corner or guest room. Choose kitchen and bathroom spots near plumbing lines to cut cost. Place bedrooms in quieter corners away from street noise. If Vastu is important, add a small prayer spot in a quiet north east or north corner. Small planning now avoids changes and delays later.

Design Tips for Comfort

Use windows for cross ventilation to keep rooms cool and fresh. Add a small north facing balcony or verandah for morning light. Choose light colored flooring and wall finishes to reflect light. Use built in storage and lofts to save floor space. Plan power points and lighting where furniture will be placed. Keep the kitchen ventilated so cooking heat can leave the house.

Material and Budget Notes

Use the plan to list main materials like bricks, cement, tiles, and paint. Ask your builder for a cost estimate based on the drawing. Check local prices and keep a small buffer for changes. Use standard sizes for doors and windows to save cost. A clear plan reduces waste and helps control the budget.
