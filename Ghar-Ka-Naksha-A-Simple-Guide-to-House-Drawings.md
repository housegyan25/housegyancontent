What is in a House Drawing

A house drawing has a floor plan and room layout. It shows where doors and windows will be. It also shows bathroom and kitchen locations. For two storey homes it shows stair layout. The drawing helps with material list and cost estimate.

How to Choose the Right Plan

Measure your plot. Make a list of required rooms. Think about light and air. Think about the position of kitchen and bathrooms. If you follow simple Vastu tips, mark the prayer room and main door direction. Small changes at plan stage can save big money later.
