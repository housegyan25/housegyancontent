What an East Facing Plan Shows

A clear plan marks the living room, bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms, and doors. For an east facing plot the living room or verandah can face east to welcome morning sun. Windows are placed to bring light and cross ventilation. The plan helps you see room sizes and where furniture will fit. It also helps your builder make a material list so construction runs smooth.

Steps to Pick the Right East Facing Layout

Start by noting your plot size and the direction it faces. Think about how your family uses rooms in the morning and evening. Place living or prayer space to enjoy early light. Keep bedrooms in quieter corners away from street noise. Put the kitchen where plumbing is easy and where fumes will not enter living areas. If you want a pooja corner, place it in the east or northeast part of the home. Small choices at this stage save time and money later.

Design Tips for Comfort

Use windows for cross ventilation to keep rooms cool. Add a small verandah or balcony in the east to use morning sun. Choose light colored flooring to reflect light and keep rooms bright. Use built in storage to save floor space. Plan sockets and lights near likely furniture spots. Keep gas and meter in safe areas with easy access.
