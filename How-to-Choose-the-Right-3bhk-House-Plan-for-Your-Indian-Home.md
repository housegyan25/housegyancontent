What a 3BHK plan includes

A clear plan shows three bedrooms, a living room, a dining space, a kitchen, and one or two bathrooms. It gives room sizes and door points. It marks window spots for good light and airflow. For two storey homes it shows stair position. The plan helps you place furniture and storage.

Steps to pick your plan

Start with your plot size and direction. This helps decide where windows get sun. List family needs like a study, guest bed, or store. Choose kitchen and bathroom spots to cut plumbing cost. Place the living room near the main door for guest access. If Vastu matters, add a small prayer corner in a quiet area. Small choices now save time and money later.

Design tips for comfort

Make the living room bright with morning light. Add windows for cross ventilation in bedrooms and kitchen. Use built in storage to save floor space. Keep clear paths for easy movement. Plan sockets and light points where you need them. Place gas and meter in safe spots. These choices make daily life easier and safer.

Material and budget tips

List main materials like bricks, cement, tiles, and paint from the plan. Ask your builder for a material estimate using the drawing. Check local prices and keep a small buffer for changes. A clear plan helps reduce waste and control cost.

Before you start building

Share the final drawing with your builder and ask for a quote. Keep a printed copy on site so workers can follow the plan. Update the drawing if you need changes before work starts. Use the plan for permits and local checks. A final approved drawing keeps work smooth and avoids delays.
