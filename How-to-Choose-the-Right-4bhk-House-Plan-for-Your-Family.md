What a 4BHK Plan Includes

A clear 4BHK plan shows four bedrooms, a living room, a dining area, a kitchen, and two or more bathrooms. It gives room sizes and door points. It marks window positions for light and cross ventilation. For two storey homes it shows stair and landing placement. The plan helps you place furniture and storage and makes the material list for the builder.

Steps to Pick Your Plan

Start with your plot size and direction. This helps place windows for morning sun and shade. List your family needs like a study, guest room, or extra store. Choose the kitchen and bathrooms so plumbing works stay short. Place the living and dining area centrally to make family time easy. If Vastu matters, add a small prayer room in a quiet corner. Small choices now avoid big changes later.

Design Tips for Comfort

Make key rooms get good light in the day. Add windows and vents for cross ventilation in bedrooms and kitchen. Use built in storage to free floor space. Plan wide paths for easy movement between rooms. Place sockets and lights near furniture spots. Keep the gas and meter in safe, easy to reach spots. These choices make daily life easier and safer.

Materials and Budget Notes

Use the plan to list main materials like bricks, cement, tiles, and paint. Ask your builder for a cost estimate from the drawing. Check local prices and keep a small buffer for changes. A clear plan helps reduce waste and control cost on site.
