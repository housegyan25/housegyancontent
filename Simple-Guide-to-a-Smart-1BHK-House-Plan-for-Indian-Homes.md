Why a plan matters

A good plan saves time and money. It shows where rooms go and how to use space. For small homes a clear plan stops mistakes during building.

Key parts of a 1BHK plan

Living room first. The living room is the main area for guests. Keep it near the entrance.


Bedroom next. The bedroom should be quiet and get good airflow.


Kitchen close to the living room. Make it easy to move food and dishes.


Bathroom position. Put the bathroom near the bedroom for comfort and short pipes.



Space saving tips

Use sliding doors to save space. Add shelves high on walls. Choose furniture that can do two jobs. For example, a sofa with storage. Use light colors to make rooms look bigger.

Simple vastu and climate tips

In India, many people follow vastu. Keep the main door clean and bright. Place the kitchen in the southeast if possible. Use windows to get cross ventilation. This helps in hot months.
