Main Parts of an Elevation
The front elevation must show the main door, windows, balcony, roof lines, and finish materials. It should give heights and show any steps or railings. This helps the builder match the design on site and avoid guesswork.
Choose Materials with Care
Materials decide the look and the cost. Paint is cost friendly. Tiles or stone give a strong look but add to cost. Wood finishes add warmth but need maintenance. Pick materials that suit your budget and weather. Light colors help in hot areas.
Light and Airflow Matters
Design the front so rooms get good daylight. Add small overhangs to block heavy sun and rain. Place windows to allow cross ventilation. These choices make rooms more comfortable and reduce cooling needs.
Work in Steps with Your Designer
Share plot width and photos with the designer. Tell them your style and budget. Review the draft elevation and ask for small changes. After approval, give the final Home Front Elevation Design to your builder and start work.
