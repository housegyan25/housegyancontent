A good <a href="https://www.housegyan.com/service/house-drawings-and-2bhk">2bhk House Plan</a> is the first step to a comfortable home. It shows where rooms go and how people will move inside. This guide helps you pick the right plan for small families and city plots.
Know Your Needs
List who will live in the house now and in future. Do you need space for guests or elders? Do you want a study corner or storage for items? Make a clear list before choosing the plan.
Layout Ideas
Open plan living and dining saves space. Bedrooms should be private and quiet. Place the kitchen near the utility area and bathrooms to save plumbing cost. Use windows on two sides of the living room for good airflow and light.
Practical Tips
Use sliding doors to save space. Add shelves and built in closets to reduce clutter. Choose durable and low maintenance materials. Plan a small balcony or planter box to bring light and plants.
Work with the Designer and Builder
Share your plot size and style choices. Ask for a plan that shows exact room sizes. Check the plan on paper and in photos. Make changes early. After approval, give the plan to your builder and keep a copy on site.
