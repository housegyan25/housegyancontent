What a Good 2BHK Plan Includes

A good plan shows two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, and one or two bathrooms. It gives room sizes and door points. It marks window positions for light and air. For two storey plots it shows stair position. The drawing helps you check if furniture will fit and if rooms get enough light. It also helps your builder make a material list.

Steps to Choose Your Plan

First, know your plot size and direction. This helps place sun facing windows. Second, list family needs like a study corner or guest bed. Third, pick where the kitchen and bathrooms will sit. Keep bathrooms near plumbing to cut cost. Place the living room near the main door for guest access. If you follow Vastu, add a small prayer corner in a quiet spot. Small planning now saves time and money later.

Design Tips for Comfort

Make the living room bright with morning light. Add windows in bedrooms for cross ventilation. Use built in shelves to save floor space. Keep clear paths for easy movement inside the house. Plan enough sockets and light points. Also place the gas and meter in safe, easy to reach spots. These choices make daily life easier and safer.
